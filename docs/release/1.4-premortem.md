# Unli Rice 1.4 (Mac) — 6-Month Post-Mortem, run as a premortem (2026-10-29)

The gate in `_AI Context/07_Prelaunch_Post_Mortem.md`, run before Submit for Review. Written as
if 1.4 shipped in November 2026 and failed by May 2027, then working back. The founder chose to
archive first and run this before submitting (2026-10-29).

## Context & data

1. **The product.** 1.4 reorganises the Mac app around "guardrails, grounding, evals"
   (INTENT-007). The sidebar shows two jobs, Grounding and Guardrails. Projects, To do, Map, Your
   year so far, Separate memories, PDF/ZIP export and the Capture card move behind switches in
   Setup › Extras. Nothing is deleted. **No new capability ships**: the memory-files view
   (Phase B) and evals (Phase C) are not built.
2. **Audience and value.** Developers and heavy AI users who run several assistants (Claude,
   Codex, ChatGPT) and want one place for what those assistants know and must follow.
3. **Pricing.** Free, open source, MIT. No account, no IAP.
4. **The outcome (imagined).** 1.3 users update and find Map and Your year so far gone from the
   sidebar. New users read "Guardrails, grounding, evals" under the app name and look for evals
   that don't exist. Reviews call it "a settings app for a memory nobody writes to". Downloads
   are flat; nobody turns Extras back on because nobody knows they're there.
5. **Assumptions.** That a simpler sidebar reads as progress; that users who relied on Map or
   Year would find Setup › Extras; that the narrowed promise (inspect memory files, check session
   logs) is worth installing for before it exists; that the App Store listing would be updated to
   match. **Unknown:** real usage of 1.3. The only readable event log covers two days in July 2026
   (724 events, 697 from ingest, 22 from Claude); the installed app's own counts are unchecked.

## 1. The core paradox

The audience says it wants "one memory across my AIs", but by late 2026 every assistant remembers
on its own, and the evidence that agents write to Unli Rice is thin (22 Claude writes against 697
imported events in the only readable log). 1.4 answers that by *removing* things, while the
reason to stay (seeing every assistant's memory, checking whether it followed your rules) lands
in later phases. A release that only takes things away needs a reason the user can feel on day
one. 1.4 has a cleaner sidebar and a tagline it can't keep yet.

## 2. Distribution & friction

- **Discovery.** The listing still sells Brain Map, Profiles and Looking Back
  (`APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md`), and two of the eight screenshots show Map and Your year so far.
  A new user installs, finds neither, and leaves. That is also an App Review Guideline 2.3
  accuracy risk.
- **Upgrade.** 1.3 shipped the old sidebar (Repos, To do, Your year so far, Map;
  `feature/todo-widget:ContentView.swift`). Phase A keeps Projects and To do for existing
  installs, but Map and Your year so far default off, so a 1.3 user who used them loses the door
  without being told where it went.
- **Time to value.** Unchanged and still the main drop-off: connecting an assistant means pasting
  a config block into another app's file. 1.4 does nothing for it.
- **Copy that contradicts itself.** The sidebar says "Guardrails, grounding, evals"; Home still
  says "One memory your AI tools share. Tell something to Claude, and ChatGPT knows it too"
  (`HomeView.swift:48-52`). Neither is quite what 1.4 does.

## 3. The paywall / conversion trap

There's no price, so the cost is attention and trust: granting folders, pasting configs, and
believing an app that just hid half its features. The "conversion" that fails is the second
launch. With nothing new to try, a returning user has no reason to open the app after updating.

## 4. Blind spots & flawed assumptions

- **"Evals" is promised before it exists.** The sidebar subtitle names a job with no screen.
- **The listing is part of the release.** Description, screenshots and reviewer notes all name
  features that 1.4 hides by default (`APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md` reviewer note: "Brain Map …
  100% usable directly within the standalone GUI").
- **Usage is unmeasured.** The overhaul's keep/cut list was made without the real note counts
  the intent asked for. Hiding Map may be right, but nobody knows how many people used it.
- **One app-boundary check is still open.** Adding a folder with import off was never seen in a
  running app (plan Build notes).

## 5. Key takeaways & anti-patterns

1. **Don't ship a promise ahead of its screen.** Change the sidebar subtitle for 1.4 to
   "Guardrails and grounding" (or keep "AI Notes & Memory") until evals exist.
2. **A hide-only release must say where things went.** What's New names Map, Your year so far
   and Separate memories and points to Setup › Extras in one line.
3. **The listing ships with the build.** Rewrite the description, replace the Map and Year
   screenshots with the new sidebar and Setup › Extras, and update the reviewer notes to say how
   to turn the extras on.
4. **Measure before cutting further.** Check real counts in the installed app before Phase B, as
   the intent already asks.
5. **Don't treat 1.4 as the overhaul.** It's the clean-up that makes room for it. Say that in
   the roadmap page rather than in the App Store.

## Verdict

Shippable **after** takeaways 1–3. Takeaway 1 is a one-line code change and means rebuilding the
archive (still 1.4 (9), since nothing has been uploaded). Takeaways 2 and 3 are text and images,
done in this release's other files. Takeaways 4 and 5 don't block 1.4.
