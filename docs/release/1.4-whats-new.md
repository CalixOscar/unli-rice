## Mac — Unli Rice 1.4

1.3 was never submitted (App Store Connect still held it in Prepare for Submission on 2026-10-29), so
the live version is 1.2 and this text also covers 1.3's To Do widget. Pasted into App Store Connect
on 2026-10-29, with the draft version changed from 1.3 to 1.4.

A calmer Unli Rice, built around two jobs: what your AI knows, and the rules it should follow. Plus a To Do widget for your desktop.

• The sidebar now has two sections. Grounding holds your notes and the folder your AI can read. Guardrails holds your House Rules, what needs your decision, the Trust Center and archived notes.
• Everything else is still here, under Setup › Extras. Turn on Projects, To do, Map, Your year so far, Separate memories, PDF and ZIP export or the iPhone Capture card whenever you want them. Turning one off only hides it; nothing is deleted.
• If you already used To do or your repos, Projects and To do stay on after the update. Map and Your year so far are now in Setup › Extras.
• New To Do widget. Add it to your desktop to see what your AI assistants suggested doing later, oldest first, and tick the circle when something is done.
• Your AI assistants now write to-dos you can act on: a plain title, and what you need to do yourself or whether an AI can handle it.
• Two background jobs have their own switches: importing files from your folders, and spotting duplicate notes. Turn one off and that job stops, including when you press Ingest now or the janitor buttons. Claude Code sessions are still imported either way.
• The sidebar scrolls instead of overflowing on smaller windows.
