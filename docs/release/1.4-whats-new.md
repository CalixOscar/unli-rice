## Mac — Unli Rice 1.4

A calmer Unli Rice, built around two jobs: what your AI knows, and the rules it should follow.

• The sidebar now has two sections. Grounding holds your notes and the folder your AI can read. Guardrails holds your House Rules, what needs your decision, the Trust Center and archived notes.
• Everything else is still here, under Setup › Extras. Turn on Projects, To do, Map, Your year so far, Separate memories, PDF and ZIP export or the iPhone Capture card whenever you want them. Turning one off only hides it; nothing is deleted.
• If you already used Projects and To do, they stay on after the update. Map and Your year so far are now in Setup › Extras.
• Two background jobs have their own switches: importing files from your folders, and spotting duplicate notes. Turn one off and that job stops, including when you press Ingest now or the janitor buttons. Claude Code sessions are still imported either way.
• The sidebar scrolls instead of overflowing on smaller windows.
