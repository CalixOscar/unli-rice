# Unli Rice 1.3 — App Store text (2026-09-26)

Paste into App Store Connect → the 1.3 version → "What's New in This Version".
Plain words on purpose: the reader is anyone, not a developer.

## Mac — Unli Rice 1.3

A To Do list you can keep on your desktop.

• New To Do widget. Add it to your desktop to see what your AI assistants suggested doing later, oldest first, and tick the circle when something is done.
• The To Do screen now speaks plainly. Long notes shrink to one line, with the details a click away, and every item says who suggested it and when.
• Your AI assistants now write to-dos you can act on: a plain title, and what you need to do yourself or whether an AI can handle it.
• Works out of the box. To-dos from any connected AI show up straight away, and adding your code folders in Repos also shows work that isn't backed up yet.

## iPhone and iPad — Unli Rice Capture 1.3

• New To Do widget for your Home Screen. See what your AI assistants suggested doing later and tick things off. When Unli Rice is locked with Face ID, the widget keeps your to-dos hidden.
• The To Do screen now shows every to-do your AI assistants suggested, in plain words.
• Capture now catches up with your Mac whenever you come back to it.
